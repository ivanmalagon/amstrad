# Traducir — Translation

## Instructions

1. Ask Ivan to paste the entry to translate, or specify which entry from `Dev journal/`.
2. Read `Idiomatic clues` below to load the idiomatic reference list before translating.
3. Translate following the writing style and formatting rules below.
4. Output the English translation directly, so Ivan can copy-paste.
6. If any phrase is ambiguous or colloquial and no idiomatic clue covers it, stop and ask Ivan before guessing. Suggest adding the resolved expression to `Idiomatic clues`.

## Writing style

- **Honest and self-aware, not self-pitying.** Ivan names his ego reactions, his doubts, his frustrations — directly, without dressing them up. Don't sanitize this in translation.
- **Narrative, not report.** Preserve the arc of the entries.
- **Colloquial but sharp.** Casual register, precise thinking. Not LinkedIn. Not a postmortem. Something in between.
- **Short paragraphs.** Match the rhythm of the original.

## Formatting rules

- No em-dashes (—). Use a comma, a period, or rewrite the sentence.
- No Oxford pretension. Write like a person, not a style guide.
- Preserve paragraph breaks from the original.
- Do not add a title unless the original has one.
- Do not add a conclusion or summary that isn't in the original.

## Idiomatic clues

- Rallada de la semana -> This week's gnawing rat

- Está de puta madre -> it's badass

- La peña -> Folks

- Una fusilada -> shamelessly stolen

- Bajona -> ask Ivan. Options depending on intensity: "that deflated me a bit" (mild), "that gave me a little gut punch" (stronger)

- Cambió la película -> that changed everything
