## What I'm up to now

### Working at Omnia

I joined [Omnia](https://www.useomnia.com/) mid-2025 as founding engineer. It feels very meta: using AI on a dialy basis to develop AI agentic flows to improve AI positioning.

It's a challenging industry. Everything seems to change every 3 days. Both in my field in general (product engineering), and in how AI products change and how that reflects in positioning.

I've progressed orders of magnitudes since I joined. After a long stressful period in my previous gig, I was not sure if I needed to chill out or to go full throttle. I chose the latter and it's proven to be the best decision. I'm having LOTS of fun.

### Fitness

After discovering the [Shovelglove system](https://shovelglove.com/) last December, I've changed my approach to exercise. Not by using a sledgehammer, though. I found the 14 minutes, every normal day, simple routine, focus on maintentance compelling. I've simplified my routine to a boring one (pushups, pullups, squats), doing 10 to 20 minutes everyday (leaning towards 10 minutes, to be honest) and, surprise! I'm seeing results I didn't see in the previous months.

I think it's just the "be consistent and do the same routine", instead of trying to top my reps, and changing routines every two weeks. Consistence is key seems to be true.
